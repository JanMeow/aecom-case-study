# PB-04 Incident Command and Communications

## 1. Purpose and scope
Who leads, who must be told, and what must be reported during a storm event affecting SGW's power or water systems.

## 2. Incident command
- The Emergency Manager on duty (see the on-call roster) is Incident Commander until relieved.
- Section leads: T&D Operations (power), Water Operations (water and wastewater), Field Operations (crews), Communications, Regulatory Affairs.
- The VP Operations is briefed at least every 6 hours while the event is active.

## 3. Incident room
1. Open an incident room when any asset is rated Critical, or when two or more assets feeding critical facilities are rated High.
2. Members: the Incident Commander, the owner of each Critical asset, and the owners of assets downstream of it.
3. Every decision is logged in the room with time and decision owner.

## 4. Situation briefing (every 6 hours, or when the situation changes)
A briefing must cover:
1. Current storm position, intensity and forecast for the service area.
2. Assets at High and Critical risk, with customers and critical facilities affected.
3. Dependencies: which power failures would take down water or wastewater assets.
4. Actions taken and actions pending, with owners.
5. Resource status: crews, mutual aid, generators, fuel.
The Incident Commander approves each briefing before it is distributed.

## 5. Public communication
- Only the Communications Manager releases public statements, after Incident Commander approval.
- Boil water notices and outage estimates go out through SGW's website, social media and local media.

## 6. Regulatory reporting
- Florida Public Service Commission: outage reports as requested during the event (typically daily).
- Florida DEP: sanitary sewer overflows within 24 hours.
- County health department: boil water notices, same day.
- NERC/FERC: reportable events on the bulk power system per the applicable reporting standards.
Regulatory Affairs prepares all regulatory reports; the AI assistant may draft but never sends them.

## 7. Critical customer contacts
Hospitals, emergency operations centres and nursing homes on an affected circuit are called directly by the regional supervisor before landfall, and again when their supply is lost or restored.
